# S3-20eo: exact address32 absolute SIB disp32 Dword store

## Scope and semantics

This runtime slice admits only the eight-byte address-size-32 singleton
`67 89 04 25 id` (`MOV [disp32],EAX`): the address-size override, ModRM/SIB
with no base or index and scale 1, Dword effective-address size, and Dword
transfer. The decoder-owned disp32 is reduced modulo 2^32 and zero-extended
to form the absolute effective address. The store writes the low 32 bits of
RAX in one contiguous checked four-byte access. For disp32 `-2`, the span
starts at `0xffff_fffe` and extends through `0x1_0000_0001` without per-byte
32-bit wrap. The guest instruction boundary is `A+8`. No noncanonical-address
or `#SS` claim is made for this addr32 form. Neighboring prefixes and
encodings remain outside the exact admission.

The JIT source/site fixture is
`memory_load_v2::tests::ir_dword_addr32_absolute_sib_disp32_store_binds_all_source_bytes_to_its_site`.
It covers six disp32 representatives, binds all eight source bytes to the
site and `A+8` boundary, and rejects mutations to opcode, ModRM, SIB,
displacement bytes, and forged site fields. The host fixture is
`tests::s3_20eo_addr32_absolute_sib_disp32_dword_store_uses_zero_extended_absolute_address`.
It checks the one contiguous width-four write at `0xffff_fffe`, the low32 RAX
payload, architectural no-commit on injected backend failure followed by
successful retry, entry STOP/budget guards without writes, and callback STOP
after commit with re-entry rejected and no second write.
