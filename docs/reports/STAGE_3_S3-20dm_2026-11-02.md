# S3-20dm — exact address-size-32 no-displacement SIB dword stores

**Status:** bounded implementation slice; S3-20 and Stage 3 remain open.

Runtime commit `7866706` adds exactly four four-byte singleton forms:
`67 89 04 0B`, `67 89 04 4B`, `67 89 04 8B`, and `67 89 04 CB`
(`MOV [EBX+ECX*1/2/4/8],EAX`). The decoder-owned address-size-32 effective
address adds low32 EBX to low32 ECX times the selected SIB scale, wraps to 32
bits, then zero-extends before canonical-address checking. The high halves of
RBX and RCX are ignored. Each store commits low32 RAX through one contiguous
checked four-byte write. Source binding and the PC-map boundary at `A+4`
distinguish the exact prefix and scale. Fixtures cover errors, STOP, and
budget behavior.
