# S3-40f — allocator decision ledger addendum

**Status:** bounded allocator evidence and its limits are recorded; the current baseline remains. This addendum does not close S3-30 or S3-40 and does not create a new architecture decision.

## Evidence entered in the ledger

S3-30a and S3-30b compare two configurations of the same existing register-home allocator: production-baseline `register_homes=4` and diagnostic `0` (spill-only). Budget0 is not a new allocator algorithm or an established improvement. The two profiles are recorded separately because their frozen branch fixtures and samples differ; their results must not be pooled or treated as a before/after progression.

S3-40a was a historical ledger snapshot at source revision `8759b92`; its statement that no timed allocator alternative A/B was available was accurate at that point. This S3-40f addendum records later evidence without rewriting that historical status. S3-30a's convergent `JZ +0` profile and S3-30b's distinct `JZ +10` RIPs are not a controlled comparison of branch-routing overhead and do not show continuous allocator improvement.

Correctness evidence remains narrower than a general allocator proof:

- [S3-30p3](STAGE_3_S3-30p3_2026-11-01.md) closes the sum-only observation gap for the existing helper-clobber probe: it checks six simultaneously live W64 values (four register homes and two spill/reload values) after each of two wrapper calls. It preserves the existing `21` return contract and canaries; it is a bounded transport probe, not arbitrary live-value coverage.
- [S3-30p4](STAGE_3_S3-30p4_2026-11-01.md) checks a separate pending-flags JZ/JNZ fixture across `register_homes` 0..4 and budgets 0..4, both branch results, captured ADD operands/helper paths, home/spill placements, entry STOP, and STOP/budget continuation without prefix replay. The block has four definitions; homes4 assigns all four to homes with zero ordinary value spills. Entry STOP proves no retirement and no helper call, not zero generated-entry calls. This is one W64 fixture and its admitted continuation, not all pressure, paths, operations, or workloads.

## Decision and next evidence

The existing fixed-home allocator configuration at `register_homes=4` remains the baseline. No evidence here supports changing it, declaring budget0 better, selecting another allocator algorithm, or changing a production default. The measured ratios overlap parity for the taken branch and compilation; the fallthrough ratios are above parity on this fixture, but that observation does not establish a general advantage or disadvantage.

The next allocator decision gate needs a representative hot workload and a specific alternative motivated by that workload. Compare the alternative with the current baseline on identical frozen inputs and conditions, retain repeated raw samples and timer boundaries, and keep full-state correctness, compile cost, code size, and warm execution in view. If no concrete alternative is justified by a representative profile, retain the simpler current allocator and document that result rather than promoting diagnostic budget0.

The evidence does not close broader S3-30 or S3-40 work, select policy for other workloads, or support owner/MMU/SMC claims. Production memory ownership and MMU integration are outside this fixture scope. The full Stage 3 and physical/Mac mini gates remain open; Mini checks remain deferred until the end of Stage 3.
