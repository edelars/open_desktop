# S3-20fd: address32 scaled RAX-indexed no-base SIB Dword load

## Scope

This runtime slice adds the exact eight-byte forms
`67 8B 04 45/85/C5 disp32` for address32 Dword loads at SIB scales 2, 4, and
8. There is no base; RAX is the index. The effective address uses the old low
32 bits of RAX:
`zero_extend_64((low32(original RAX) * scale + sign-extended disp32) mod
2^32)`. Product and addition wrap at 32 bits. A contiguous checked four-byte
read writes EAX, zero-extends it into RAX, preserves other GPRs and RFLAGS,
and on success commits guest RIP `A+8`. A read beginning near 4 GiB remains
contiguous past that boundary; only the effective-address calculation wraps.

Runtime source `faaac0f` is integrated with test-lifetime follow-up `7bc7995`.
The follow-up binds the compiled block before taking its borrowed memory-site
reference, keeping the owner alive for the duration of the assertions. The
[normative JIT contract](../specs/JIT_CONTRACT.md#s3-20fd-exact-address32-scaled-rax-indexed-no-base-sib-dword-load)
records the exact admission and semantics.

## Static review evidence

Independent Sol/high static review passed after the borrow-lifetime fix. The
review covered the exact prefix/opcode/SIB profile, scales, decoded addr32
effective address, displacement and source-byte binding, original-RAX use,
EAX zero-extension, and `A+8` boundary. The host fixture source compares
native and interpreter state for representative signed displacements and
low32 wrap cases; it also contains entry-guard and budget retries,
backend-error retry, and callback STOP/no-replay cases.

These are source-review observations, not executed results. No behavior tests,
builds, formatting, Cargo checks, Python harness, or machine smoke were run.
The exact JIT/host gates remain pending. Harness S3-34co pins those
identities; its independent review passed after restoring the distinct
34cn/34co fail-closed assertion tails, but the harness was not executed. See
the [S3-34co report](STAGE_3_S3-34co_2026-11-02.md) and
