# S3-30e strict dead-home reuse on M1 Pro

Strict dead-home reuse reduced warmed direct-entry time by 1.72% across
the five measured fixtures. The pressure fixture took 4.66% less time,
and the combined median for the four control fixtures was near parity.
Complete compilation took 0.58% more time by the paired median. These
are the same five cases used for the signed correctness and emission
checks. [Source patches and correctness](STAGE_3_S3-30d_2026-11-20.md)

| Warm direct entry | 60 | 26.1108 ns | 25.6623 ns | 0.982805 [0.970737–0.988498] | 537.646 ms |

Compile process medians of the paired ratio were 1.004704, 1.009895 and
1.006615. Warm process medians were 0.982795, 0.982735 and 0.982853.
The pressure case had a warm ratio of 0.953405 [0.938939–0.967935].
The four controls combined had a warm ratio of 0.999882
[0.981528–1.007829]. Each of the five fixtures contributes the same
number of calls. On the pressure case the experimental allocator emitted
20 static transfer sites, used a 256-byte frame and emitted 3,064 bytes
of code. Fixed homes emitted 33 transfer sites, used a 288-byte frame
and emitted 3,052 bytes of code. [Static attribution](STAGE_3_S3-30d_2026-11-20.md)

## Timed scope and load

Each mode accepted the first passing attempt in each of three independent
process rounds. Each process contributed 20 alternating pairs under the
eager flags policy. Both variants were in one signed ARM64 release
executable. Shared calibration targeted 400 ms per combined side, with
a 200 ms minimum in collection. Compile used 16,384 calls per fixture
per side and consumed the whole compiled artifact. Warm direct entry used
4,194,304 calls per fixture per side, after compilation, publication and
the correctness checks. The direct-entry interval calls the published
block with prepared guest state. The ordinary cache and dispatcher are
part of the separate untimed readiness check.

Compile preflight rejected two attempts with median idle 86.65% and
86.70% against a 90% floor. Three more compile attempts had overlapping
idle minimums of 73.2%, 74.1% and 73.3% against an 80% floor. Each
accepted compile process had a timed idle minimum of at least 86.1%,
and each accepted warm process had at least 82.6%. All nine timed
processes ran as ordinary UID 501 with effective nice −5.

## Allocator decision boundary

The measured direct-entry benefit motivated the paired comparison through
the ordinary warmed FIFO16 dispatcher on the same inputs. That follow-up
retained fixed homes for the measured production profile. A promotion to
strict reuse would require candidate-specific forced helper failure and
asynchronous STOP checks. [Ordinary FIFO16 allocator decision](STAGE_3_S3-30f_2026-11-20.md)
