# S3-40i — cached dispatcher phase attribution ledger addendum

## Acceptance

Add the completed S3-31b phase attribution to the provisional measurement
ledger, retaining its frozen workload and observed timing boundaries. Do not
interpret phase shares as a production profile or make an implementation
decision from a net observer-path delta.

## Result

S3-31b compared ordinary and explicit-observer full cached-dispatch calls on
the six synthetic S3-36f register-loop strata. Three release processes retained
60 pairs per stratum, and all 720 timed sides passed the 200 ms floor. The
recorded phases were block build/admission, first key/snapshot/lookup, and the
host execution boundary. Across the strata, their median observed-call shares
were 21–33%, 39–44%, and 4–6%, respectively. The remaining observed-call time
contains unmeasured validation/control/retirement work and observer hooks.

The observed/ordinary median ratios were 1.0900–1.1182; the `b_64` raw paired
range crossed parity. These differences include instrumentation and
timing/path noise and are not isolated observer cost. The phase shares include
the timer observer, apply only to two synthetic kernels, and do not measure
total lookup cost or pure generated-code execution. The data establish no
linking benefit, production default, or runtime/cache policy change. S3-31,
S3-36, S3-40 and S3-41 remain open; Mini checks remain deferred to the end of
Stage 3. See the [S3-31b report](STAGE_3_S3-31b_2026-11-01.md) and
[evidence manifest](data/stage3/s3-31b-20261101/suite-manifest.json).
