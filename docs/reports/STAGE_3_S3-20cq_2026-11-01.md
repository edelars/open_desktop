# S3-20cq — exact aliased Dword store

**Date:** 2026-11-01. **Status:** implementation and focused local checks complete; independent review passed with no actionable findings.

## Scope

The shared typed-IR Dword-store path now accepts the existing exact `89 03`
(`MOV [RBX],EAX`) and one new exact encoding, `89 00` (`MOV [RAX],EAX`). The
decoder-provided full old RAX is used both as the effective address and, through
its low 32 bits, as the store payload. RAX is unchanged. The path still emits
one checked four-byte write and retains the Dword-specific artifact and compile
flavor. No additional encoding family or owner/MMU/cache/SMC/dispatcher path was
added.

## Local evidence

- Compiler admission covers both exact encodings and rejects other ModRM bytes,
  prefix variants, SIB/displacement forms, incomplete and trailing source. The
  alias descriptor is tied to RAX, `A+2`, Dword operand size and exact source
  bytes; a forged base or changed source is rejected.
- Native host fixture compares `89 00` with the interpreter for full CPU/RAM
  state at an address above 4 GiB and at `0xffff_fffe`, checks the single exact
  four-byte callback and adjacent sentinels, and verifies RAX remains unchanged.
- The fixture checks entry budget zero, successful write followed by callback
  STOP (`A+2`, retired=1), exact callback payload on architectural/backend write
  errors, and noncanonical RAX #GP without callback or state/RAM mutation.
- The PC map remains a single-site map ending at `A+2`; HelperCall,
  AfterHelper and commit snapshots are inadmissible. A known all-or-nothing
  helper error retains its replay-current-instruction exit; post-commit states
  cannot replay. Existing `89 03` coverage continues to exercise its full
  success, guard, STOP and fault cases.
