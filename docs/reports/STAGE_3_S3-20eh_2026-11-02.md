# S3-20eh — exact address-size-32 ESP-base dword load with signed disp8

## Scope

S3-20eh admits only the exact five-byte singleton `67 8B 44 24 ib`
(`MOV EAX,[ESP+disp8]`) through the shared typed-IR checked-load path. It uses
ESP as the SIB base, no index, scale 1, and a signed 8-bit displacement. The
effective address is `(low32(RSP) + sign_extend(disp8)) mod 2^32`, then
zero-extended. High 32 bits of RSP are ignored. The checked read consumes four
contiguous guest-address bytes; individual bytes do not wrap at 4 GiB. EAX is
zero-extended into RAX and RIP advances to `A+5`.

The host fixture checks wrap at ESP zero and `u32::MAX`, high32-poisoned RSP,
interpreter state/RAM parity, one width-four read, and a successful span
crossing the 4-GiB boundary. Callback STOP occurs after the read commits; a
retry at the committed RIP is rejected without a second read. These fixtures
do not establish production owner/MMU, physical-hardware, or performance
behavior.
