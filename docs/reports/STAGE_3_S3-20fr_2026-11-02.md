# S3-20fr: RCX-base Dword load signed disp8 `8B 41 ib`

## Planned scope and semantics

This slice plans to admit exactly the three-byte form `8B 41 ib`
(`MOV EAX,[RCX+sign_extend(ib)]`): Dword operand, Qword address size, no
prefix, index, or SIB. The effective address is the full original RCX plus
the signed 8-bit displacement, wrapping modulo 2⁶⁴; ECX is not used. The
operation performs one contiguous checked four-byte read. The value
zero-extends into full RAX; RCX, other GPRs, RFLAGS, and RAM remain unchanged;
successful retirement advances RIP to `A+3`.

Canonicality and range preflight apply to the computed EA and the complete
read range. Existing guest/backend faults, entry guards, retry, and callback
STOP without replay remain part of the contract. RCX disp32, addr32, RDX-base,
other destinations, prefixes, and SIB forms are outside this slice.

## Planned implementation and coverage

The implementation is expected to add exact compiler metadata, a separate
`CheckedDwordLoad` literal-byte gate, decoded/IR/runtime length3/i8 address
profiles, and exact source/site binding. Existing `[8B,00]` and `[8B,03]`
forms must remain supported as distinct sites. Previously rejected
`[8B,41,00]` and `[8B,41,FF]` forms must become successful compilations with
source/site mismatch checks against the corresponding original forms.

Rust identities:

- JIT: `memory_load_v2::tests::ir_dword_rcx_disp8_load_binds_all_signed_source_bytes_to_its_site`
- Host: `tests::s3_20fr_rcx_disp8_dword_load_uses_full_rcx_and_signed_displacement`

Harness identities:

- `s3_34dc_ir_rcx_disp8_dword_load_source_binding`

- Fail-closed self-test: `test_s3_34dc_rcx_disp8_dword_load_gates_are_pinned_and_fail_closed`

Expected harness totals after both slices: 284 Rust gates (274 acceptance and
10 lifecycle), 302 local steps, and 126 self-test names. The final harness
gates must match actual Rust test definitions and argv.

Planned host coverage includes signed displacement edges, full RCX versus a
low32 decoy, zero and unaligned effective addresses, canonical upper-half
addresses, wrapping arithmetic near `u64::MAX`, and rejection of a noncanonical
computed EA or overflowing full read range before the read callback. Faults
must preserve state and allow retry; every entry guard must have a successful
retry case; callback STOP must not replay the read. JIT coverage should bind
all 256 displacement bytes, verify signed metadata and the `A+3` PC-map
boundary, and reject forged site metadata.
