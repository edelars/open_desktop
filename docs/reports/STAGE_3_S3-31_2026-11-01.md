## Timer boundaries and interpretation

The outer timer wraps the full `Variant::call` or `Variant::call_observed`
adapter plus dispatcher call, including CodeView, dependency and
control/context setup. State cloning/reset, full result/state validation,
PhaseRecorder construction/reset, and phase-total extraction are outside each
timed interval. The host execution phase includes backend/arena checks, host
context construction, generated entry, and result decoding; it is not pure
guest execution time.

`key_snapshot_and_initial_lookup` covers minimum sizing, TranslationKey
construction, initial snapshot validation, the first `cache.find`, and the
hit/miss counter update only. A cache hit performs a second `cache.find`,
cached snapshot and code-view revalidation, guards, accounting, and additional
cache bookkeeping outside this phase. Those costs, adapter work, and recorder
bookkeeping outside phase clock samples remain in the residual. Thus the
reported key phase is not total lookup cost, and the residual is not
linking-removable dispatcher overhead.

The observed/ordinary ratios are only the net difference between the two full
call paths, including recorder bookkeeping and possible generic-instantiation
differences. They are not a pure observer-cost estimate or a performance
winner. These two fixtures and this phase split do not establish a linking
benefit or a general dispatcher result.

No per-pair or system-load telemetry was retained for any run, so Stage 2
Cargo/rustc activity or overlap with individual samples cannot be established.
None of the runs waited for a quiet window, and no Stage 2 process was stopped. No
sample was excluded: all 20 pairs in each stratum are retained in each run.
The broader S3-31 acceptance remains open.
