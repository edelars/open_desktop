Each raw sample batches 45, 46, or 47 corpus sweeps. The table reports the

## Measurement limits

`corpus-cold-sweep` begins each measured sweep with an empty code cache, but
cache entries may be reused later in that same sweep. The timer accumulates
dispatch-call intervals. Fixture/trace parsing and admission, interpreter
oracle, arena/cache construction and destruction, state reset, and result
validation are outside the timed intervals. The process launches once per
report; these data are not cold-process startup measurements.

The interpreter was an oracle outside timing; the legacy fragment path was not
timed. No broader helper-heavy, memory, fault, or fallback workloads beyond
the exact frozen corpus are represented. Host thermal telemetry was not collected. Therefore
these observations do not support a full first-execution or warm-runtime
comparison, a speed claim across engines, a cache-policy/default decision, or
performance acceptance for Stage 3. Full cold/warm/resource gates remain open.
