# S3-20en: exact address32 absolute SIB disp32 Dword load

## Scope and semantics

This runtime slice admits only the eight-byte address-size-32 singleton
`67 8B 04 25 id` (`MOV EAX,[disp32]`): the address override, ModRM/SIB with no
base or index and scale 1, Dword effective-address size, and Dword transfer.
The decoder-owned disp32 is reduced modulo 2^32 and zero-extended to form the
absolute effective address. The checked read is one contiguous four-byte
access; for disp32 `-1`, it begins at `0xffff_ffff` and extends through
`0x1_0000_0002` without per-byte 32-bit wrap. EAX zero-extends into RAX and the
guest instruction boundary is `A+8`. No noncanonical-address or `#SS` claim is
made for this address-size-32 form. Other prefixes and neighboring encodings
remain outside the exact admission.

The JIT source/site fixture is
`memory_load_v2::tests::ir_dword_addr32_absolute_sib_disp32_load_binds_all_source_bytes_to_its_site`.
It covers nine signed disp32 representatives, verifies decoded address and
transfer sizes, binds all eight source bytes to the site and `A+8` boundary,
and rejects forged site fields and source-byte mutations. The host fixture is
`tests::s3_20en_addr32_absolute_sib_disp32_dword_load_uses_zero_extended_absolute_address`.
It checks a single width-four read at `0xffff_ffff`, EAX zero-extension,
preservation of the other GPRs and RFLAGS, entry STOP/budget guards without a
read, backend-read failure without architectural commit followed by retry,
and callback STOP after commit with re-entry rejected and no second read.
