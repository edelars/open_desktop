# S3-20fk: exact address32 RAX-alias Dword store

## Scope and semantics

This bounded runtime slice admits only the three-byte singleton `67 89 00`
(`MOV [EAX],EAX`). The effective address is zero-extended low32 of
pre-instruction RAX and the payload is low32 of that same original RAX. One
contiguous checked four-byte write preserves full RAX, other GPRs, and RFLAGS;
successful retirement advances RIP to `A+3`. Writes beginning at
`0xffff_fffd`, `0xffff_fffe`, and `0xffff_ffff` continue contiguously across
4 GiB without wrapping individual transfer bytes.

## Source review and verification

The JIT fixture binds exact source bytes and decoded site metadata, checks the
single-instruction `A+3` PC-map boundary, and preserves distinct provenance
for supported `89 00` and `67 89 03`. At the time this slice was reviewed, its
fixture rejected RAX addr32 displacement and SIB forms. S3-20fl has since
admitted the exact signed-disp8 load form `67 8B 40 ib`; addr32 disp32 and SIB
forms remain outside that slice. The host fixture covers interpreter parity,
zero and unaligned addresses, nonzero payloads with nonzero RAM sentinels, different high32 and
`0xffff_ffff_xxxxxxxx` inputs, contiguous boundary-crossing writes, precise
CPU/RAM state, STOP/budget/entry guards, architectural/backend failures with
retry, and callback STOP without replay. The host publisher retains its
existing owning MAP_JIT lifecycle around execution.

review over `0834f3f..afa89ba`; no concrete findings remain. Review covered the
separate `CheckedDwordStore` literal whitelist and decoded-form gate, IR
address predicate, bounded runtime site profile and byte matcher, host
