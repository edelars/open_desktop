## Timing evidence and calibration mismatch

The v4 calibration observed 11,313,582 ns of epoch-body time across only 512
epochs (22,096 ns per epoch). Its 128 report/RSS checkpoints therefore occurred
every four epochs. It measured 1.981 seconds of checkpoint time and 2.159
seconds of fixed process cost, then projected 660.000014 seconds for the
recommended count.

The scored process reports 432.315102379 seconds of epoch-body time, or
14,564.737 ns per epoch, plus 2.210876324 seconds of checkpoint time. The
remaining reported process time is 4.956014005 seconds. Recomputing each
checkpoint interval from differences in cumulative epoch-body time gives a
minimum 14,162.379 ns, median 14,541.535 ns, maximum 15,300.932 ns, and first
interval 14,235.005 ns per epoch. The sample rates are consistent across the
run. These measurements establish a mismatch between the v4 calibration sample
and this scored run; they do not identify its cause.

Process RSS was 6,062,080 bytes before runtime creation, 8,945,664 bytes after
stress and before runtime drop, and 8,896,512 bytes after drop. RSS is
process-wide auxiliary evidence; it does not establish cache attribution or
prove leak-free behavior.

## Scope and next step

This run exercises supported cache APIs with one runtime and one arena at a
time. It does not test production owner notification, guest-write SMC,
active-code modification, guest faults, simultaneous arenas, SMP, or
leak-free behavior. The v4 run remains useful for its completed sequence,
parity, exact counters, occupancy, and auxiliary resource snapshots, but it
does not meet the sustained-duration target and must not be combined with v5
measurements. Mini checks remain deferred to the end of Stage 3.
