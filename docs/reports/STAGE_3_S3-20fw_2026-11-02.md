# S3-20fw: Address32 RCX-base Dword store singleton `67 89 01`

## Scope and contract

This slice is limited to the exact three-byte form `[67,89,01]`:
`MOV [ECX],EAX`, with `67` as the only prefix, Dword data and address sizes,
RCX as the addr32 alias base, and no index or displacement.

The effective address is zero-extended `low32(original_RCX)`; high RCX bits do
not participate. The stored payload is `low32(original_RAX)`. Execution must
perform one contiguous checked four-byte write. Full RAX, RCX, other GPRs and
RFLAGS remain unchanged; successful next RIP is `A+3`. The starting address is
32-bit, while bytes after `0xffffffff` continue above 4 GiB without bytewise
wrap. Existing guest/backend failures, guards, retries, and callback STOP
without replay remain in scope.

The admission change requires a separate addr32 RCX singleton predicate. It
must be included in all three store-admission OR groups, with corresponding
typed-IR union, runtime source/site profile, `CheckedDwordStore` literal-byte
gate, and host publisher whitelist. Do not add RCX to the broader shared RAX
or RBX addr32 profiles.

- Host: `tests::s3_20fw_addr32_rcx_base_dword_store_uses_zero_extended_ecx_and_low_eax_payload`

Harness identities:

- `s3_34dh_ir_addr32_rcx_base_dword_store_source_binding`
- `s3_34dh_host_addr32_rcx_base_dword_store_zero_extended_ecx_and_low_eax_payload`
- Fail-closed self-test: `test_s3_34dh_addr32_rcx_base_dword_store_gates_are_pinned_and_fail_closed`

JIT coverage should pin the exact bytes, widths, base/index/scale/displacement,
PC-map boundary `A+3`, forged metadata, and distinct positive source/site
bindings for supported neighboring forms. Host coverage should vary high32
RCX while keeping ECX equal, including noncanonical full64 values; distinguish
the low32 target from a full64 decoy; and cover zero, unaligned, and boundary
starts `FFFFFFFD/FE/FF` with a contiguous write across 4 GiB. It should also
verify independent RAX payloads, payload edge values, guest/backend failures
with retry, each entry guard with a successful retry, and callback STOP without
replay. Fetch errors must propagate without a fallback value.

Two stale compile-negative fixtures using `[67,89,01]` are expected to become
successful compilation plus bidirectional source/site mismatch checks: the
RAX-alias singleton fixture and the RCX addr64 singleton fixture. Keep
`[67,89,01,80]` rejected as a trailing-byte form, along with other unsupported
prefix, operand, base, and SIB forms.

The harness baseline was 292 Rust gates (282 acceptance and 10 lifecycle), 310
local steps, and 130 self-test names. The integrated pair now has 294 gates
(284 acceptance and 10 lifecycle), 312 steps, and 131 self-test names. The
exact Rust identities and harness gate identities are confirmed. Harness
commit `4cc94ba` passed independent Sol/high review. Runtime commit `8fa1400`
is integrated and its independent review is pending.
