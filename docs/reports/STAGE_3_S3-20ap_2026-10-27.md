# S3-20ap report: address32 direct EBP-base stack-data MOV

## Scope and implementation

Added exact singleton `67 48 8B/89 /r disp8|disp32` forms with ModRM
`mod=01/10,r/m=101`. The decoded base is guest RBP, its low 32 bits form the
address, data registers are RAX..RBP, and the transfer remains a full qword.
Instruction lengths are 5 bytes for disp8 and 8 bytes for disp32. The
`mod=00/rm=101` form is rejected as a separate absolute/decoder form; no SIB,
extra prefix, pair, or V3 admission was added.

Direct V2, shared IR, runtime metadata/source validation, and bounded
dispatcher compile-at matching now recognize the same profile. The decoder's
`MemoryOperand` and `access_address_48` remain the only address semantics.
Stack-data helpers only widen the exact EBP-base exception; generic GPR rules
remain unchanged.

## Evidence

`address32_rbp_direct_base_has_exact_qword_profile` checks both operations,
all six data registers, signed disp8 boundaries, and signed disp32 boundaries.
It compares direct V2 and shared IR metadata/source bytes, checks decoded EBP
base and instruction length, and rejects mod00/rm101, SIB, extra/reordered
prefixes, unsupported data registers, trailing bytes, and partial windows.

`address32_direct_rsp_rbp_data_keeps_qword_values_and_wraps_contiguously`
now also exercises the EBP-base form across all six data registers and both
operations against the interpreter. It checks full state/RAM parity, exact
callback targets and counts, high-half truncation, modulo-2^32 addition and a
contiguous qword crossing 4 GiB. RBP-as-data/base alias cases preserve old-RBP
addressing and full-width store payload semantics.

The host test also checks dispatcher budget split and pre-entry STOP; a
noncanonical RBP loaded through the address32 form commits once, then a
64-bit RBP-based access raises #SS without a second callback. Exact-profile
load/store backend errors do not commit state or RAM. A successful EBP-base
store commits before callback STOP. Partial disp32 fixture windows and
`mod=00/rm=101` do not publish, retire or call memory.
