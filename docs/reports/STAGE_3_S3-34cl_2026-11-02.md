no-base-SIB Dword store forms with scales 2, 4, and 8:
`89 04 45/85/C5 disp32` (`MOV [RAX*scale+signed disp32],EAX`). The two
required Rust identities are the JIT source/site gate
`memory_load_v2::tests::ir_dword_rax_index_no_base_sib_scaled_disp32_store_binds_all_source_bytes_to_its_site`
and host gate
`tests::s3_20fa_rax_index_no_base_sib_scaled_disp32_dword_store_uses_scaled_rax_address_and_low_dword_data`.
Their harness IDs are
`s3_34cl_ir_rax_index_no_base_sib_scaled_disp32_dword_store_source_binding`
and
`s3_34cl_host_rax_index_no_base_sib_scaled_disp32_dword_store_rax_address_and_data`.

The harness self-check pins both IDs and exact identities in the registries,
manifest, invocation and required-evidence validation. It checks that missing
gate declarations or passing evidence fail closed. This report records harness
source and static review only.
