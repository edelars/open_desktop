# S4-14f: INVLPG with CR0.WP=0/1 in the diagnostic session

**Date:** 2026-11-06
**Scope:** an opt-in INVLPG CPU profile and its feature-gated mutable-session
integration. Parent S4-14 and Stage 4 remain open.

## Behavior

`InvlpgWpLong64V1` / `step_invlpg_wp` admits the same exact bare `0F 01 3B`
(`INVLPG [RBX]`) instruction as the existing `InvlpgLong64V1`, under CR0
`0x80010033` (WP=1) or `0x80000033` (WP=0). Decode, privilege, fetch, fault,
noncanonical no-op and typed invalidation-effect semantics share one internal
executor. The existing `step_invlpg` remains WP=1 only.

`MutableTranslationProbeSession::execute_invlpg_wp` first checks CPU CR0/CR3/CR4
against its snapshot. It fetches through the pure page walker, bypassing the
resident cache. A canonical retired invalidation clears the single entry before
return; a noncanonical retired NOP, fault, unsupported form or context mismatch
preserves the resident cache. This entry does not adopt MOV CR0's post-retirement
poison rule. MOV CR3 remains WP=1 only.

## Coverage and limits

Tests exercise WP=0 and WP=1 invalidation with a resident stale hit, then a
post-effect walk and hit at the changed physical mapping. They also cover
noncanonical NOP cache retention, exact Ring1–3 #GP(0), LOCK #UD, fetch fault,
unsupported encoding, CR0/CR3/CR4 mismatch, preservation on rejected paths,
unchanged page-table entries across instruction fetch/effect consumption.
A dedicated fetch-cache regression warms a matching `AccessKind::Fetch` entry
at INVLPG RIP, removes that RIP's PTE, verifies the exact fetch #PF and zero
retirement, then confirms the stale Fetch entry remains resident. The operand
address is not dereferenced; this remains a one-entry diagnostic experiment,
not a production TLB. Tracked A/D caching, reset, other control
register transitions, PCID/global behavior, JIT integration and SMP remain out
of scope.
