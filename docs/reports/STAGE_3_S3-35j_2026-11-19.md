# S3-35j Cold FIFO phase attribution on M1 Pro

The signed ARM64 release of `9405ff4` ran `cold-phases` on the Apple M1
Pro with macOS 14.6.1 and Rust 1.99.0. The relocated input list and exact
historical S3-17 trace bind the same 1,010 inputs and 1,322 visits as
S3-35i. The ordinary and observed dispatcher paths agreed on state,
exit, retirement, budget and cache counters. Each sweep started with a
fresh FIFO16 runtime and recorded two hits and 1,320 publications.
[Full first-execution measurements](STAGE_3_S3-35i_2026-11-19.md)

Three processes contributed 60 ordinary/observed dispatch pairs. All
120 intervals cleared 200 ms. The smallest ordinary and observed
intervals were 200.458 ms and 206.461 ms. The processes ran under UID
501 at nice −5. Separate 24-probe preflights admitted each accepted
process. The lowest idle sample overlapping an accepted process was
82.2%. The first attempt at round three was rejected at 74.6% idle.

## Dispatch and phase results

Ordinary dispatch took a median 8.371 ms per sweep. Observed dispatch
took 8.633 ms. The median paired observed/ordinary ratio was 1.0315
with a range of 1.0131–1.0577. Ordinary initialization, measured on its
own, took a median 0.877 µs per sweep. The observed sweep medians for
attributed phases and other dispatch time were 7.674 ms and 0.957 ms.

## Reproduce

Use `cold-phases --relocated-s3-17-paths` with the recovered list and
historical trace. Restore the archive's `files` into a scratch directory
and run `python3 -B kit/run.py combine attempts-v2` to reproduce
`audit.json`. The archive binds the binary SHA-256, input manifest,
host and load receipts, raw timing reports and rejected attempt.
