# S3-35b — cold FIFO pipeline attribution

**Status:** three release-process runs completed on the frozen S3-17
register-only corpus. This adds bounded timing attribution to S3-35a; it does
not select an optimization, cache policy, or default. Full Stage 3, Mini,
physical x86, memory/helper workloads, and resource gates remain open.

## Correctness and phase events

Every ordinary and observed sweep checked the result against the prepared
interpreter oracle. It compares all `LongModeState` fields and masks only the
fixture-declared undefined RFLAGS bits; exit, remaining budget, retirement, and
block count must also match. The ordinary and observed preflight additionally
compares the complete CPU state and `DispatchResult` for every case, plus all
cache and arena counters.

Each preflight passed with 1,010 calls, 2,990 retired guest instructions,
1,322 native block executions, 2 cache hits, and 1,320 misses, successful
compiles, and publications. In every accepted cold sweep the event counts
matched: 1,322 block-build/admission, key/snapshot/lookup, and host-execution
events; 1,320 each for lowering, allocation, emission/finalization, and
publication. The two hits still paid block construction and key/lookup work.

## Results

The dispatch totals below are medians and ranges across the 60 accepted
samples, normalized by each sample's sweep count. Initialization is recorded
separately; teardown, case-state cloning, interpreter-oracle work, and
post-call result checking are outside dispatch intervals.

| Path | Median per 1,010-case sweep | Range across samples |
|---|---:|---:|
| Ordinary FIFO16, no-op observer | 6.752 ms | 6.391–7.149 ms |
| Observed FIFO16 | 6.986 ms | 6.601–7.260 ms |

The paired observed/ordinary dispatch ratio across all 60 pairs had a median
of **1.0301**, an interquartile range of **1.0261–1.0342**, and a full range of
**0.9831–1.1075**. This paired ratio captures total observation overhead and
run-to-run noise; it does not isolate the cost of a clock read. Ratios below
1.0 occur within the observed spread, so no precise overhead or speed claim is
made from the ratio alone.

Phase intervals use timestamps taken by `PhaseRecorder::begin` and
`PhaseRecorder::end`. A phase spans from the `Instant` sample in `begin` to the
clock read in `end`; it includes the wrapped operation and callback/wrapper
transit between those samples. Recorder bookkeeping after the end sample is
outside that phase. The table reports the median per-event interval across the
60 accepted samples, with the IQR and full range of those sample means. Event
counts are per complete corpus sweep. These intervals are attribution on the
same observed path; do not add their medians as an exact whole-path time.

| Key, snapshot, and lookup | 1,322 | 417.03 | 401.64–420.69 | 393.88–426.20 |
| IR lowering | 1,320 | 425.62 | 411.57–431.55 | 407.54–437.62 |

`other_dispatch_ns` records control guards, cache installation/accounting,
result validation, and observer work outside the measured phase intervals. Its
median was 1.164 ms per sweep (range 1.109–1.196 ms). It is not compiler time.
Initialization was separately reported in each raw sample; runtime/cache/arena
teardown remains outside the dispatch intervals and was not timed separately.

## Timing scope and limits

Observation uses the real FIFO16 dispatcher, compiler, backend publication,
and executor boundary. The measured phase intervals are sequential and
non-overlapping. Each cold sweep creates a new runtime/cache/arena; ordinary
and observed sweeps use the same prepared frozen cases and interpreter checks.
Calibration alternates ordinary and observed sweeps. Pair order alternates,
and each accepted pair has the same sweep count on both sides. Run 2's short
pair triggered a full-series restart, as specified by the harness.

The per-phase intervals include the timer boundary transit described above;
the overall paired ratio is the observer-overhead calibration. The benchmark
does not subtract timer overhead from individual phases. These measurements
describe one bounded cold register-only corpus on this host and do not compare
independently calibrated phase samples with the S3-35a whole-path report as an
exact sum. No optimization/default decision follows from this slice. Mini,
Stage 2, physical x86, and owner/MMU were not part of the clean run windows.
