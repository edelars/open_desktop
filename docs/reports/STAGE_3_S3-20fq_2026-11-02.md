# S3-20fq: RCX-base Dword store singleton

## Scope and semantics

The planned slice admits only the two-byte form `89 01` (`MOV [RCX],EAX`),
with no prefix, displacement, or SIB. The operand is Dword and the address
size is Qword. The effective address uses the full original RCX; the write
payload is the original low32 RAX. Execution performs one contiguous checked
four-byte write. Full RAX, RCX, the other GPRs and RFLAGS remain unchanged on
success, and RIP advances to `A+2`.

Existing canonical48 and range preflight, guest/backend faults, entry guards,
retry, and callback STOP without replay remain part of the contract. RCX
disp8/disp32, addr32 aliases, SIB, other bases/sources, and extra prefixes are
outside this slice.
