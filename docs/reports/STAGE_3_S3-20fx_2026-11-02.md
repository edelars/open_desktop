# S3-20fx: Address32 RCX-base Dword load signed disp8 `67 8B 41 ib`

## Scope and contract

This slice is limited to the exact four-byte form `[67,8B,41,ib]`:
`MOV EAX,[ECX+signext(disp8)]`, with `67` as the only prefix, Dword data and
address sizes, RCX as the addr32 alias base, no index, scale 1, and one signed
8-bit displacement.

The starting effective address is
`zero_extend((original_ECX + sign_extend(ib)) mod 2^32)`. The complete original
RCX is preserved. Execution performs one contiguous checked four-byte read;
the loaded value is zero-extended into the complete RAX, other GPRs and RFLAGS
are preserved, and successful next RIP is `A+4`. Only calculation of the
starting address wraps at 32 bits: transfer bytes after `0xffffffff` continue
above 4 GiB without bytewise wrap. Existing memory/backend faults, entry
guards, retries, and callback STOP without replay remain in scope.

The accepted form does not include RCX disp32 (`67 8B 81 id`), indexed SIB,
other bases or destinations, or additional prefixes. Supported neighboring
forms `[67 8B 01]`, `[8B 41 ib]`, `[67 8B 40 ib]`, and `[67 8B 43 ib]` remain
distinct positive source/site bindings.

Rust identities:

- JIT: `memory_load_v2::tests::ir_dword_addr32_rcx_disp8_load_binds_all_signed_source_bytes_to_its_site`
- Host: `tests::s3_20fx_addr32_rcx_disp8_dword_load_uses_wrapped_ecx`

Harness gates:

- `s3_34di_ir_addr32_rcx_disp8_dword_load_source_binding`
- `s3_34di_host_addr32_rcx_disp8_dword_load_wrapped_ecx`
- Fail-closed self-test: `test_s3_34di_addr32_rcx_disp8_dword_load_gates_are_pinned_and_fail_closed`

The JIT fixture binds every one of the 256 displacement bytes to this exact
source/site, checks signed displacement metadata, PC-map boundary `A+4`,
source/site mismatch in both directions, and forged fields. The host fixture
covers signed disp8 and ECX address wrap, preservation of full RCX, one
contiguous checked Dword read, RAX zero-extension, and existing fault/guard
retry and callback STOP-without-replay behavior. A follow-up correction sets
the original RCX to `0x1234567800000100` and the full64 decoy start EA to
`RCX−128`, or `0x1234567800000080`, removing its accidental alias with the
low32 target used by the addr32 case.

Two stale compile-negative fixtures are expected to become successful
compilation with bidirectional source/site mismatch checks:

- `[67,8B,41,00]` in the addr32 RCX singleton fixture;
- `[67,8B,41,80]` in the addr64 RCX disp8 fixture.

Keep disp32, indexed SIB, unsupported prefix/base/destination, trailing-byte,
and truncated-source cases rejected.

Before this slice, the harness baseline was 294 Rust gates (284 acceptance and
10 lifecycle), 312 local steps, and 131 self-test names. After integration it
has 296 gates (286 acceptance and 10 lifecycle), 314 steps, and 132 self-test
names. Runtime commits `930e4c5` and `ce0701d`, and harness commit `c5224aa`,
are integrated. Runtime and harness passed independent Sol/high review.
