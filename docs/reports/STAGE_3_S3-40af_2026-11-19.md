# S3-40af Adjacent x87 comparisons in interpreter RUN on M1 Pro

Both builds use `8c159c8` and the S3-40z public RUN driver.
The candidate groups `Fcompp`, `Fucompp`, `Fucom` and `Fucomp`
in one `matches!` admission check. The four operations already
used the same helper with source index 1 and the same unsupported
outcome. The grouped check keeps that helper at the same point
between the adjacent admission gates. S3-40ae had already changed
the `Fucomp` comparison.
[Previous interpreter optimization](STAGE_3_S3-40ae_2026-11-19.md)

In the signed S3-40ac image, the three remaining adjacent equality
checks appeared in sampled predecoded STEP paths. The original STEP
had 1, 0 and 1 samples at the respective directly verified callers.
The shared STEP had 5, 9 and 3. The historical build also executed
these checks. The current experiment measures the grouped predicates
on ordinary public RUN.

Disassembly of the signed M1 Pro binaries followed the timed public RUN
to its STEP specialization for `FixtureMemory`. It confirmed a change
from 93 to 90 static `Operation::eq` calls and the disappearance of all
three selected calls. The two untimed M1 checks covered the 2,400 frozen
MOV inputs at budgets 0, 1 and 2 and the separate zero-budget
unmapped-code case. Both local builds passed 2,609 `od-isa` tests,
including eight focused x87 cases.

## Results

Six paired rounds alternated build and sequence order. Each process
used all six permutations of budgets 0, 1 and 2. The fixed counts
from S3-40z were 4,374,000 calls per interval for store then load and
4,339,200 for load then store. The series retained 432 intervals
and 1,882,051,200 RUN calls. The shortest interval was 103.250 ms
against the 45 ms floor.

Time fell by 3.17% and 2.58% at budget 1 and by 3.28% and 3.14%
at budget 2 by paired medians. All six rounds improved across
the two sequences. The zero-budget medians changed by 0.32%
and 0.24%. These are whole-call measurements of the two linked
builds, including their code layout. The grouped check is used
in the interpreter. ISSUE-521 remains open for the historical gap.

## Environment and reproduction

MacBookPro18,3, Apple M1 Pro, eight logical CPUs, macOS 14.6.1
(23G93), Xcode 15.4 (15F31d), Rust and Cargo 1.99.0, and Python 3.12.5.
The signed ARM64 release builds used the macOS 14.0 deployment target.
Both passed strict signature verification with Hardened Runtime and
`allow-jit`. The machine was on AC power. All twelve scored processes
recorded UID 501, nice −5 and QoS `0x21`.

The 24 preflight probes ranged from 84.2% to 99.1% idle CPU,
with a median of 96.65%. The 39 in-run probes ranged from
81.4% to 86.8%, with a median of 86.4%.

archives. Follow the archived `README.md` for the release builds,
