# Stage 4 S4-32c — freestanding stack→UART probe

Статус: bounded slice accepted after independent `gpt-6.1-sol/high` review with
no P0–P3 findings. This report is not a claim that the parent S4-32 or Stage 4
is complete.

## Contract

`Stage4StackProbeV1` uses the exact freestanding entry bytes `51 58 EE F4`:
`PUSH RCX; POP RAX; OUT DX,AL; HLT`. The artifact owns one payload qword at raw
offset `0x8000` / GPA `0x9000`. The strict loader passes that qword into `RCX`,
starts `RAX` at zero and `RSP` at `0xa01000`, and reuses the S4-32b Long64/CPL0,
CR3/EFER, page-table and COM1 handoff.

After four retired instructions the expected state is: payload at GPA `0xaff8`
and in `RAX`, restored `RSP=0xa01000`, one UART byte equal to the payload low
byte, `RIP=0x400004`, and guest `Halted`. The opt-in
`run_paged_stack_probe` quantum selects `PagedCpl0StackProbeV1` and owns fetch,
decode, page walking, RAM effects and PIO; the legacy `PagedCpl0ProbeV1`
remains unchanged. The loader and runner do not inject the result or duplicate
those semantics.

## Verification plan and boundaries

The focused tests cover two payloads, reproducible builder output, budgets 0…4,
split calls, stack canaries, halted repeat no-op, corrupted code/page-table
artifacts, legacy-profile rejection, A/D write ordering, exact page-fault
records, full CPU snapshots on faults, noncanonical #SS, cross-page refusal,
backend failure, POP-only A/D behavior, STOP after committed PUSH and POP with
resume to HLT, and negative opcode/prefix cases.

Not claimed: full stack restart/rollback semantics, exception delivery or CR2
commit, two-page transactions, IRQ/APIC, HLT wakeup, JIT, Linux, privileged
hardware, parent S4-32, S4-33, or Stage 4 acceptance. ISSUE-054 remains open.
