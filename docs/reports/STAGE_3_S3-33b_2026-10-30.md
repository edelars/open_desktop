# Stage 3 S3-33b: exact native DEC RAX

## Result

Added a separate opt-in, uncached native path for exact bare W64 `DEC RAX`
(`48 FF C8`). `S0_09NopDecRaxAdmission` is separate from the unchanged
INC-only `S0_09NopIncRaxAdmission`; compile and dispatch expose separate eager
and demand-flags candidate entry points. No shared cache identity or default
policy changed.

DEC lowers as W64 Subtract by constant one. Its partial flag mask updates
PF/AF/ZF/SF/OF and preserves CF and all untouched RFLAGS bits. The demand-flags
candidate materializes a pending full writer before DEC and saves/restores live
allocator homes across that helper boundary. DEC flags are merged eagerly; no
new pending descriptor or helper ABI was added.

## Coverage

- Exact admission accepts only bare `48 FF C8` and rejects truncation,
  prefixes, INC, DEC to RCX/RBX/R8, immediate SUB, memory DEC, and branches into
  the DEC encoding. The INC-only and NOP-only admissions continue to reject DEC.
- Compiler coverage includes following native JZ and PC-map boundaries.
- Host differential coverage compares full CPU state and retirement against
  `od_isa::execute::step` for CF=0/1 and inputs 0, 1, `u64::MAX`, signed
  extrema, and AF/PF edges. Both JZ and JNZ paths are taken across the cases.
- Eager and candidate native dispatch, candidate budgets 0..6, STOP at entry
  and immediately before DEC after pending CMP, and pending-flags PC-map
  evidence are covered. Each full native candidate retires the selected six
  guest instructions natively.

## Limits

Stage 2 sources or fixtures, production `od-machine`, ABI/cache policy, physical
x86, or Mini. It makes no performance claim. Mini and performance checks remain
