# Stage 3 S3-33g: exact native NOT RAX

Date: 2026-10-30  
Status: bounded implementation; independent code review and final local harness passed. Final report/evidence review passed.

## Scope

This slice adds an opt-in uncached native path only for bare `48 F7 D0`
(`NOT RAX`). Admission requires the exact decoder form and instruction
boundaries, including rejection of branch targets into the instruction. The
lowering models the value operation as `u64::MAX - RAX`, writes RAX, and
commits once with `FlagEffect::Preserve`. Eager and demand-flags candidate
compile/dispatch APIs use the existing IR and statepoint contracts.

Emitter acceptance for this preserve operation is limited to W64 `Subtract`
with a result. NOT does not call the flags helper, write flags or their
definedness, stage flags, or create/replace/clear a pending-flags descriptor.
An earlier pending writer's saved operands remain intact. No IR, ABI,
interpreter, Stage 2, `od-machine` owner/MMU, Mini, or performance changes are
included.

## Evidence

- Admission rejects neighboring group/register/memory/width/prefix/truncation and
  in-instruction branch-target forms, and remains separate from NEG.
- Host differential tests compare complete state and retirement for `0`,
  `u64::MAX`, signed extrema and alternating-bit values under four distinct
  arithmetic-flag patterns; all non-RAX registers are initialized to canaries.
  Eager and candidate paths agree with the interpreter.
- Pending CMP and ADD → NOT → JZ/JNZ cases choose branches opposite to the
  flags that a mistaken `u64::MAX - RAX` flag writer would produce. A two-NOT
  pending-CMP sequence, NOT without a prior writer, and NOT → ADD are covered.
  STOP and budgets are checked before NOT and before the branch after NOT.
- The compiler verifies eager/candidate PC maps and no `BLR` in NOT's code.
  The emitter test checks zero helper calls at 0, 1, 2, and 4 register homes,
  and rejects another Preserve integer form.
