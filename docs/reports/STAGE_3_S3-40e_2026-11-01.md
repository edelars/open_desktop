# S3-40e — bounded checked-memory timing evidence ledger

**Status:** this documentation addendum records three completed bounded
measurements. It does not close S3-36, S3-40, S3-41, or any physical hardware
gate, and it selects no production policy.

## Evidence scope

S3-18e is the checked-memory cache prerequisite, not a performance result. Its
committed `MemoryPairCache` implementation
(`b1a6ccb130844c40a396155a8f84bda656bfa8c3`) is limited to frozen
`StoreThenLoad` and `LoadThenStore` profiles, capacity one, and fixture-only
private ordinary RAM. It does not implement production RAM/MMU ownership,
committed-writer/SMC/DMA coherence, or a general cache policy. See the
[S3-18e implementation report](STAGE_3_S3-18e_2026-11-01.md).

The timed results below use the same two frozen S3-34a checked-memory sequence
identities and input digest
`b09478b556aa91234d8c453f20e70f637222c5de2d7369d305ffe7c78caae540`, but the
native timed intervals differ. Each profile covered 2,400 inputs total across
the two sequences and performed correctness preflight before scoring; the
source reports retain the detailed parity counts and validation fields:

| Slice | Native timed boundary | Retained paired ratios | Median and range |
|---|---|---:|---|
| [S3-35c](STAGE_3_S3-35c_2026-11-01.md) | Cold direct entry: compile + publish + execute + drop `PublishedCode` and IR per input. Interpreter execution is separately timed; reset and validation are outside both timers. No dispatcher or cache reuse. | 60 total across the two sequences | 71.4765 (66.7736–93.9423) |
| [S3-36d](STAGE_3_S3-36d_2026-11-01.md), `store_then_load` | One resident published block; direct typed entry. No dispatcher lookup or cache activity. Reset and result validation are outside timers. | 60 | 1.3718847445 (1.3116083380–1.4203506060) |

Every ratio is raw paired `native_total_ns / interpreter_total_ns`; a ratio
above 1 means the interpreter side took less time for that pair. S3-35c reports
60 corpus-level pairs, each covering both named sequences. S3-36d and S3-36e
each preserve 60 pairs per sequence, without combining their strata. All three
reports record three release processes and a 200 ms minimum measured-side
floor; all scored sides passed. S3-35c used 20 pairs per process across its
two sequences. S3-36d and S3-36e used 20 pairs per sequence per process.

These are different native timed paths. In particular, neither
`S3-36e − S3-36d` nor `S3-36d − S3-35c` is a cache-cost estimate: the paths,
setup/lifetime work, and in the S3-35c case the aggregation boundary differ.
The evidence cannot be subtracted into cache-only cost or a cache
break-even point. Do not treat the ratios as a common cold/warm progression.
In S3-36e preflight, each sequence had 2,399 hits and one initial miss,
compile, and publication with a resident high-water of one entry; scored pairs
had no misses, replacements, compiles, or publications.

## Limits and decision

These results cover only the two frozen checked-memory fixture sequences.
They do not establish general memory performance, a production default or
winner, owner/MMU integration, SMC behavior, broader workloads, or whole-system
performance. No result closes the broader S3-36 dispatcher/lookup or workload
matrix, S3-40 decision work, S3-41 acceptance, or physical x86/hardware gates.
Mac mini checks remain deferred until the end of Stage 3. Concurrent activity
recorded in monitoring snapshots has no per-pair timestamps and cannot be
assigned to individual measurements; this does not stop Stage 2 work or alter
the retained measurements.
