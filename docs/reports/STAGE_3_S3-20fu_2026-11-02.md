# S3-20fu: RCX-base Dword store signed disp32 `89 81 id`

## Implemented scope

This slice admits only the six-byte form `89 81 id`:
`MOV [RCX+disp32],EAX`, with a Dword operand, Qword address size, RCX base,
no index, and no prefix or SIB. The displacement is a signed little-endian
`i32`, sign-extended into site metadata. The effective address is computed
from the full original RCX with signed displacement modulo 2⁶⁴. The payload
is the low 32 bits of the original RAX.

Execution performs one contiguous checked four-byte write. All GPRs,
including full RAX and RCX, and RFLAGS remain unchanged; successful next RIP
is `A+6`. Canonicality and range preflight apply to the computed EA and full
write range. Existing guest/backend faults, entry guards, retry, and callback
STOP without replay remain in scope. Address32, SIB, other bases or source
registers, and prefixes are excluded.

JIT coverage binds each displacement byte to its site, checks signed
metadata and the `A+6` PC-map boundary, and forges site fields. Supported
neighboring forms remain distinct sites. Two existing compile-negative cases
for `89 81 00000000`—in the RAX disp32 store and RCX singleton store fixtures—
were changed to successful compilation with bidirectional source/site mismatch
checks. The Load compiler's rejection of Store opcodes and the addr32
ECX-base negative `67 89 81 ...` must remain unchanged.

The host fixture distinguishes full RCX from a low32 decoy and keeps the
low32 RAX payload independent from the effective address. It covers zero and
unaligned EAs, canonical upper-half addresses, signed wrap, the largest valid
range, and preflight failures without calling the write callback.
Guest/backend write errors preserve CPU/RAM and allow retry; each entry guard
has its own successful retry; callback STOP does not replay the committed
write. Displacement-byte fetch errors propagate without a substitute value
or fallback.

harness implementation, identities, and reviews are complete for this slice.
